I am Minrui Luo, currently completing my undergraduate studies at the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS, also known as Yao Class), Tsinghua University.

I will join the Ph.D. program in Machine Learning at the Georgia Institute of Technology, with the School of Mathematics as my home unit, where I will work with Professor Molei Tao.

My research focuses on the mathematical foundations of learning and statistical estimation. In particular, I am interested in the optimization dynamics of low-rank matrix factorization, random initialization and high-dimensional probability, and causal inference for partially observed matrices.

<h2 id="research-interests">Current Research Interests</h2>

Optimization and Learning Theory: training dynamics and global convergence of gradient-based methods for low-rank matrix factorization.
High-Dimensional Probability: random initialization, random matrix theory, concentration, and anti-concentration in nonconvex optimization.
Causal Inference and Matrix Completion: estimation of missing entries under multiple treatments and non-random observation mechanisms.
