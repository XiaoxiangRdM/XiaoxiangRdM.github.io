I am Minrui Luo, currently a senior student at the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS, also known as Yao Class), Tsinghua University.

I will join the Ph.D. program in Machine Learning at the Georgia Institute of Technology, with the School of Mathematics as my home unit, where I will work with Professor [Molei Tao](https://www.mtao8.math.gatech.edu/).

My research focuses on the mathematical foundations of learning and statistical estimation. I am particularly interested in optimization dynamics for low-rank matrix factorization, random initialization and high-dimensional probability, and causal inference for partially observed matrices.

<h2 id="research-interests">Research Interests</h2>

* **Optimization and Learning Theory:** Training dynamics and global convergence of gradient-based methods for low-rank matrix factorization.

* **High-Dimensional Probability:** Random initialization, random matrix theory, concentration, and anti-concentration in nonconvex optimization.

* **Causal Inference and Matrix Completion:** Statistical estimation under multiple treatments and non-random observation mechanisms.
